# Adım 1: Karakter sayfaları

Önce gerçek fotoğraflardan iki karakter sayfası üret. Sonraki bütün sahneler
bu iki görseli referans alacak. Burada acele etme: yüz benzemiyorsa tekrar üret,
çünkü hata sonraki 5 sahneye taşınır.

## Umut karakter sayfası

Referans: `references/umut/` içindeki 1-3 fotoğraf (yoksa `couple_03` kırpılmış).
Kaydet: `keyframes/umut_sheet.png`

```
Using the attached photos as the face and appearance reference, create a
character design sheet of this exact man as a stylized 3D animated film character.
Keep his real facial identity: face shape, eyebrows, eyes, nose, moustache, beard
shape, hairline and haircut must clearly match the photos.

[STYLE bloğu]
[CHARACTER A — UMUT bloğu]

Layout: three views side by side on a plain light grey studio background:
front view full body, three-quarter view full body, and a close-up portrait of
the face with a gentle smile. Neutral even lighting, no text, no labels.
```

## Sibel karakter sayfası

Referans: `references/sibel/` içindeki 1-3 fotoğraf (yoksa `couple_06` kırpılmış).
Kaydet: `keyframes/sibel_sheet.png`

```
Using the attached photos as the face and appearance reference, create a
character design sheet of this exact woman as a stylized 3D animated film character.
Keep her real facial identity: face shape, cheeks, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips and
long straight dark hair must clearly match the photos.

[STYLE bloğu]
[CHARACTER B — SIBEL bloğu]

Layout: three views side by side on a plain light grey studio background:
front view full body, three-quarter view full body, and a close-up portrait of
the face with a gentle smile. Neutral even lighting, no text, no labels.
```
