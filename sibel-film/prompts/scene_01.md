## Image prompt (başlangıç karesi)

```
[STYLE bloğu]

A romantic garden at night under a sky full of stars and a soft crescent moon.
A stone path lined with rose bushes in bloom leads toward a small wooden pergola
wrapped in warm string lights. Distant city lights and a suspension bridge lit in
blue across the water on the horizon. Red rose petals drifting in the air in the
foreground, soft bokeh, light mist near the ground. No people. Wide establishing
shot, low camera, the path leading into the frame.
```

## Image-to-video prompt

```
Slow cinematic dolly forward along the stone path toward the pergola, camera
tilting gently down from the starry sky. Rose petals drift slowly across the
frame, string lights twinkle softly, stars shimmer. Calm, dreamy, no people,
no camera shake.
```
